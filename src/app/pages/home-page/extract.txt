VISIT TO JEEVITHA ANATHASHRAMA

On May 29, 2025, Syog Foundation had the privilege of visiting Jeevitha Anathashrama, a home that shelters and cares for children in need. The team donated stationery and books for 50 children aged between 6 and 15 years, helping them strengthen their learning journey. To make the day even more special, the Foundation also sponsored a wholesome dinner, filling the evening with joy, warmth, and togetherness. The smiles and laughter shared reflected the true spirit of care and compassion.
