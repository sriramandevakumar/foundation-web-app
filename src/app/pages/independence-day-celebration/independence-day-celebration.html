Independence Day 2025 – Kowdenahalli Government School

Continuing its mission, Syog Foundation also joined the Independence Day 2025 celebrations at Kowdenahalli Government School. On this occasion, the Foundation donated stationery items to the students, reinforcing its commitment to empowering young learners with essential educational resources. The event was a reminder of the importance of nurturing future citizens with the values of knowledge, unity, and progress.
