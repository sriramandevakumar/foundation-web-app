OUR MISSION


By investing in education and well-being, Syog Foundation strives to plant seeds of hope that will grow into strong, capable, and compassionate individuals. Through such initiatives, we reaffirm our commitment to creating meaningful impact and spreading kindness wherever possible.



OUR COMMITMENT TO EVERY CHILD

At our foundation, we believe that every child deserves equal access to quality education, regardless of their financial background. We don’t just provide funds, we walk alongside each child and their family to ensure they have the right environment to learn, grow, and succeed.



HOW WE SUPPORT

End-to-end care: From school fees, bus fees, books, and uniforms, we take care of the essentials so families can focus on the child’s learning.
✓
Direct impact: Fees are paid directly to schools for complete transparency.
✓
Guidance & mentoring: Monthly checkpoint discussions with every child help us track progress, address challenges, and encourage them to aim higher.
✓
Extra academic help: When a child struggles, our well-wishers step in as mentors and coaches.
✓
Partnership with schools: We regularly speak with teachers to stay updated on the child’s performance and growth.
✓
Attendance check: Students must comply with their school’s attendance requirements, which are also reviewed during our monthly checkpoints.




OUR PRINCIPLES

Supporting the most deserving: Families with a total monthly income of less than ₹20,000 are eligible.
✓
Encouraging excellence: Children are expected to maintain a minimum of 70% in all exams.
✓
Starting at the right stage: We support children from 7th standard onwards.
✓
No restrictions on faith or background: Our support is open to children irrespective of religion or caste.
✓
Balancing quality & cost: We ensure children study in schools that offer good education without unnecessary financial burden.
