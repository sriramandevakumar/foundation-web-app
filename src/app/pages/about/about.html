About Syog Foundation
Empowering Dreams Through Education and Support

At Syog Foundation, we believe that every child deserves the chance to learn, grow, and dream. Guided by this vision, we continue to support children through education, nourishment, and community engagement initiatives.

OUR PRINCIPLES

  • Supporting the most deserving: Families earning less than ₹20,000/month.
  • Encouraging excellence: Children must maintain a minimum of 70%.
  • Starting at the right stage: Support from 7th standard onwards.
  • No restrictions on faith or background.
  • Balancing quality & cost: Good schools without extra burden.

OUR COMPLIANCE

  • On track to receive the 80G tax exemption.
  • 12A certificate obtained from Income Tax department.
  • Ensures transparency and adherence to regulations.

ACHIEVEMENTS

  • One student secured 3rd rank in IIT preparation exams.
  • One student earned 10% fee concession for scoring above 80%.
  • Full support: fees, books, uniforms, transport.
  • Fees paid directly to schools.
  • Monthly checkpoints with each child.
  • Extra academic help through mentors.
  • Regular teacher communication.
  • Attendance checks as per school rules.

OUR COMMITMENT TO EVERY CHILD

By investing in education and well-being, Syog Foundation strives to plant seeds of hope that will grow into strong, capable, and compassionate individuals.

VISIT TO JEEVITHA ANATHASHRAMA

On May 29, 2025, Syog Foundation visited Jeevitha Anathashrama, a home for children in need. The team donated stationery and books for 50 children aged 6–15. They also sponsored a wholesome dinner, filling the evening with joy and warmth.
